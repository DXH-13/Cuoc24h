# Project Overview

## Ten Du An

Cuoc24h

## Y Tuong San Pham

Cuoc24h la trang web ho tro quang cao dich vu xe taxi/chay xe dich vu, ket noi khach hang voi tai xe tu do ma khong yeu cau tai xe dang ky theo cac nen tang nhu Grab, Be, Xanh SM.

Khach hang co the dat chuyen truc tiep tren website ma khong can tao tai khoan. Thong tin chuyen di se duoc ghi nhan trong he thong va gui thong bao len nhom Zalo de admin/tai xe tiep nhan.

## Van De Can Giai Quyet

- Tai xe tu do can kenh quang cao va nhan cuoc rieng.
- Khach hang can cach dat xe nhanh, don gian, khong can cai app.
- Nhom dieu phoi can nhan thong tin chuyen di moi kip thoi.
- Admin can cong cu quan ly tai xe, chuyen di va trang thai xu ly.

## Doi Tuong Su Dung

### Khach Hang

- Truy cap website bang dien thoai hoac desktop.
- Xem thong tin dich vu.
- Tao yeu cau chuyen di ma khong can dang ky.

### Tai Xe

- Dang ky thong tin ca nhan, xe va khu vuc hoat dong.
- Cho admin duyet ho so.
- Nhan thong tin chuyen di qua nhom Zalo hoac kenh dieu phoi.

### Admin

- Quan ly danh sach tai xe.
- Quan ly yeu cau chuyen di.
- Cap nhat trang thai chuyen di.
- Cau hinh noi dung thong bao va kenh Zalo.

## Pham Vi MVP

### Bao Gom

- Landing/home page gioi thieu dich vu va CTA dat xe.
- Form dat chuyen cho khach hang.
- Form dang ky tai xe.
- Backend luu tru thong tin dat chuyen va tai xe.
- Admin page co dang nhap.
- Gui thong bao chuyen moi len Zalo.

### Chua Bao Gom

- Ung dung mobile native.
- Thanh toan online.
- Dinh vi realtime tai xe.
- Tu dong ghep chuyen theo thuat toan phuc tap.
- Chat truc tiep giua khach va tai xe.
- Tai khoan khach hang bat buoc.

## Tieu Chi Thanh Cong MVP

- Khach hang co the gui yeu cau chuyen di trong duoi 1 phut.
- Admin nhan duoc thong tin chuyen moi tren Zalo.
- Admin xem va cap nhat trang thai chuyen trong dashboard.
- Tai xe co the gui form dang ky va cho duyet.
- Giao dien dung tot tren dien thoai va khong vo layout tren desktop.
